# Date rail

## What was built

A CSS-only `date-rail` organism in GRAIN (the name `timeline` already belongs to the interaction log). On desktop a hairline runs down the left of the note and each section's date is sticky inside its own section, so it pins while the reader is in that section and hands over at the section's end. At 768px and under the rail folds into a small date label above each heading. Tokens only, no shadows, no transitions, no JS.

MILL's default note layout opts in with frontmatter `layout: timeline`; each H2 written `## Aug 18 · Heading` becomes a rail section. The anchor is slugged from the heading without the date. `dateRailBody` is exported for consumers with their own layout.
